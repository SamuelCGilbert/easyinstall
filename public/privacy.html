Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

EasyInstall (easyinstall.dev) is a free tool for installing Windows apps. It collects as little as it can. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

Without signing in

The apps you tick and the presets you save are stored only in your browser (local storage). They never reach our server unless you sign in. Clearing your browser data removes them.

When you download an installer, the apps you picked are part of the download link, for example /i/vlc+7-zip. Our server uses that list to build the installer and doesn't store it.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and only used to sync your presets between devices. When you sign in, we receive from Google:

  • your Google account ID (a number that identifies your account), and
  • your email address, which is shown to you as the account your presets sync to.

We store those two things and your presets (their names and app lists). We don't receive or store your password, contacts, files or anything else from your Google account, and we never share or sell any of it.

A cookie keeps you signed in for up to 90 days. It's only used for syncing presets.

The installer

The installer runs on your PC. It downloads apps from Microsoft (winget and the Microsoft Store), Chocolatey, or the app's own developer, and writes a log file to your PC's temporary folder. It doesn't send anything about your PC back to us. Those download sources have their own privacy policies.

Hosting

EasyInstall runs on Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes requests (including IP addresses) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Open Presets while signed in and choose Delete my synced data. That deletes your account record and synced presets from our server immediately. Presets saved in your browser stay until you delete them or clear your browser data.
