What EasyInstall does

EasyInstall builds a script that installs and updates the Windows apps you pick. It's free to use. The apps themselves come from Microsoft (winget and the Microsoft Store), Chocolatey and the apps' own developers, under their own licenses and terms. EasyInstall isn't affiliated with them, and installing an app means you accept that app's license.

Use at your own risk

EasyInstall is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It installs software with administrator rights, so review what you've picked before you run it. We aren't responsible for problems caused by the apps you install, by changes their developers make, or by an install that fails or is interrupted.

Fair use

Don't use the site in a way that harms it or other people, such as flooding it with automated requests.
